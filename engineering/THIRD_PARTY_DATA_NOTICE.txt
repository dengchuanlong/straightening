Scope of derived files in this package:
The selected-B PUBLIC0007_CORE004_SELECTED_B_V002 comparison CSV/JSON combines
the reference implementation's FE predictions with OPARA specimen 0007 force and
DIC observations. Transformations include original-time-point alignment/interpolation,
force-error calculations, geometric true-strain/profile comparisons and derived summary
metrics. The FE model, its locally identified CHAB3-Voce material, comparisons and
interpretation are this study's work, not the original authors' model or endorsement.
The material identification uses selected OPARA characterization records, as documented
in the retained provenance/data_usage files. Raw characterization and specimen CSVs
are not redistributed here. Historical computed comparison files are copied unchanged;
this package performs no new FE solve or material fit.
